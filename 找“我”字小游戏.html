🔍 找“我”小游戏
Find “我” Game

游戏玩法：
1. 点击“开始游戏”后，棋盘中会出现汉字。
2. 请在棋盘中寻找并点击所有的目标字。
3. 找到所有目标字即可获胜！
How to Play:
1. Click "Start Game" to reveal characters on the board.
2. Find and click all the target characters.
3. Win the game when all targets are found!
Did You Know?
“我” (wǒ) = I / me
“找” (zhǎo) = to look for / to search
