句型

你好！ (Nǐhǎo!) - Hello!

A: 你好！(Nǐhǎo!)
B: 你好！(Nǐhǎo!)

你是学生吗？ (Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?) - Are you a student?

A: 你是学生吗？(Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?)
B: 我是学生。你呢？(Wǒ shì xuésheng. Nǐ ne?)

他也是学生吗？ (Tā yě shì xuésheng ma?) - Is he also a student?

A: 他也是学生吗？(Tā yě shì xuésheng ma?)
B: 不，他不是学生。(Bù, tā bú shì xuésheng.)

语法

Pronouns 我 (wǒ), 你 (nǐ), and 他/她 (tā)

In Chinese, pronouns like "我" (wǒ - I/me), "你" (nǐ - you), and "他/她" (tā - he/him, she/her) are straightforward. They do not change form based on gender, number, or case (subject/object), unlike in English.

我 (wǒ) - I / me
你 (nǐ) - you
他 (tā) - he / him
她 (tā) - she / her

Although "他" and "她" are pronounced identically (tā), they have different written characters to distinguish gender.

Chinese Word Order

The basic word order is SVO (Subject-Verb-Object).

我 学 中文

I learn Chinese.

你 是 学生

You are a student.

Using 是 (shì) - "To Be"

"是" (shì) is the verb "to be" in Chinese. It's used to link two nouns or pronouns, indicating identity or classification.

我 是 学生

I am a student.

他 是 老师

He is a teacher.

The Question Particle 吗 (ma)

To turn a declarative sentence into a "yes/no" question, simply add "吗" (ma) at the end of the sentence. No change in word order is needed.

你是学生吗？

Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?

Are you a student?

The Question Particle 呢 (ne)

"呢" (ne) is an interrogative particle often used to ask "what about...?" or "how about...?" It's used when the context of the previous question applies to a different subject.

我是学生。你呢？

Wǒ shì xuésheng. Nǐ ne?

I am a student. What about you?

The Adverb 也 (yě) - "Also"

"也" (yě) means "also" or "too." It is placed before the verb or adjective it modifies.

他 也 是 学生

He also is a student.

The Negative Adverb 不 (bù) - "Not, No"

"不" (bù) is used to negate verbs or adjectives. It is placed directly before the verb or adjective.

Note: When "不" is followed by a word with a 4th tone (like "是" shì), "不" itself changes its tone to a 2nd tone (bú).

不 是

Bù shì (regular tone)

Bú shì (changed tone when followed by 4th tone)

他 不 是 老师

He is not a teacher.
