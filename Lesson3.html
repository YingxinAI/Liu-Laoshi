Lesson 3: Learning Objectives

In this lesson, you will learn how to:

  • Ask and answer about nationality.
  • Ask and express the languages you can speak.
  • Use "会 (huì)" to express ability.
  • Use "也 (yě)" to express "also".
  • Recognize new vocabulary:国家、语言、美国、中国、法国、韩国、英语、中文、说、会、一点儿、也。

对话示例 (Dialogue Example - This Lesson):

小爱: 你是哪国人？ (Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? - Where are you from?)

学生: 我是美国人。 (Wǒ shì Měiguó rén. - I am American.)

小爱: 你会说什么语言？ (Nǐ huì 说 shénme yǔyán? - What language can you speak?)

学生: 我会说英语，也会说一点儿中文。 (Wǒ huì 说 Yīngyǔ, wǒ yě huì 说 yīdiǎner Zhōngwén. - I can speak English, and also a little Chinese.)
