Mahjong Learning Game: Draw & Discard Practice

欢迎来到麻将学习小游戏
Welcome to the Mahjong Learning Game

目标：组成 4 组有效组合 + 一对将牌
Goal: Form 4 valid sets + a pair. Each time you can only flip one face-down tile and discard one.

🎯 目标：
组成 4 组有效组合 + 一对将牌。每次只能翻一张隐藏牌，并打出一张无用牌。
Form 4 valid sets + a pair. You can only flip one hidden tile and must discard one useless tile each round.
📝 规则说明：
  • 每张牌最多出现 4 次。
    Each tile can appear up to 4 copies.
  • 摸一张 → 必须打一张 → 手牌 + 组合总数保持 14 张。
    Draw one → discard one → hand + sets stays at 14 tiles.
  • 胜利时显示🏆与🎆并播放掌声。
    On win, show 🏆 + 🎆 and play applause.
  • 对子：数字对子 / 同名花（梅梅/兰兰/竹竹/菊菊）/ 同名季（春春/夏夏/秋秋/冬冬）。
    Pair: identical numbers, identical Flowers, or identical Seasons.
  • 刻子：三张相同。数字/花/季都可以。
    Pong: three identical tiles (numbers/flowers/seasons).
  • 顺子：仅数字牌可组成。花/季不允许顺子。
    Chow: numbers only. Flowers/Seasons cannot form chows.
  • 听牌提示尊重已选组合，不打散已固定的对子/刻子。
    Tenpai hint respects fixed sets; selected pairs/pongs are not broken.

You won! Congratulations!
